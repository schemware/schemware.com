How five-handed works

Five players, one picker, a secret partner.

Thirty-two cards, six to each player and two to the blind. Queens, jacks and diamonds are always trump.

Deal five hands

Each player gets six cards; two go face down to the blind.

Pick or pass

Take the blind and bury two, or pass it to the next seat.

Find your partner

Whoever holds the jack of diamonds plays with the picker — nobody knows until it falls.

Take the points

The picker's side needs 61 of the 120 points to win the hand.

Friends and bots

Gather your regular table online, and let bots take any empty seat.
