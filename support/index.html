Support

Help with Sheepshead on iPhone, iPad and the web. If your question isn't answered here, email us and a real person will reply.

I didn't get the confirmation or sign-in email

Emails come from noreply@mail.schemware.com. Check your spam or junk folder, and make sure the address on your account is spelled correctly.

You can ask for a new one from the Sign in screen: choose Email me a sign-in link or Forgot your password?

I forgot my password

On the Sign in screen, choose Forgot your password? and enter your email. We'll send a link to set a new one. If you'd rather not set a password now, Email me a sign-in link signs you straight in.

I lost a device, or want to sign out everywhere

Go to Settings → Signed-in devices. You'll see each device signed in to your account, and can sign out one of them or choose Sign out all other devices.

How do I change my name or portrait?

Go to Settings → Name and portrait. Your name is what other players see at the table, in replays and on the leaderboards.

Someone played unfairly, or a bot made a bad play

Open the hand's replay and choose Report this play. Pick a reason and add a note if you like. Reports help us improve the bots and keep the tables fair.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Signed-in devices → Delete account and enter your password.

Your email, password, portrait and sign-ins are removed straight away. Your name on past games is replaced with an anonymous one, so the other players' scores and records stay correct.

If you can't sign in to delete your account, email joe@schemware.com from the address on the account.

When is Sheepshead coming out?

Sheepshead is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad. See the Sheepshead page, or email us to hear when it launches.

Contact support

Email is the fastest way to reach us. It helps to include:

  • your device (for example iPhone or iPad) and its iOS version
  • what you were doing when it happened
  • a screenshot, if you have one
